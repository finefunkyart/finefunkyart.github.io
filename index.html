Jaqulin Joseph · New Orleans

Memory takes form.

Through painting, mixed media, sculpture, photography, and found material, Jaqulin Joseph explores the body as witness—carrying ancestry, womanhood, rupture, survival, and transformation.

View selected work ↓

01

Artist statement

The body remembers what history tries to bury.

I create where memory, culture, and imagination meet. Across canvas, bronze, photography, and found material, faces and bodies become vessels for what has been inherited, survived, protected, and transformed. The materials change, but the human presence remains.
