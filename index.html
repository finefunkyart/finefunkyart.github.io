Artist statement

The body remembers what history tries to bury.

I create where memory, culture, and imagination meet. Across canvas, bronze, and found material, faces and bodies become vessels for what has been inherited, survived, protected, and transformed. The materials change, but the human presence remains.

About the artist

Jaqulin Joseph

Artist, author, photographer, and educator.

Jaqulin Joseph is a New Orleans–based multidisciplinary artist working across painting, mixed media, bronze sculpture, photography, and wearable art. Her materially expressive practice examines memory, identity, womanhood, ancestry, and the forms survival leaves behind.

She holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts and brings decades of creative practice, arts education, and community work to a body of art that asks to be felt before it is explained.
