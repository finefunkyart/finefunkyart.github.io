About the artist

Jaqulin Joseph

Artist, author, photographer, and creative force.

Jaqulin Joseph’s practice moves freely between disciplines. Her work is expressive and materially curious, guided by a commitment to tell the truth of lived experience through color, form, gesture, and image.

Across canvas, bronze, photography, found materials, and wearable art, she creates work that asks to be felt before it is explained.
