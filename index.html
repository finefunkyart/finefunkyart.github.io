About the artist

Art as witness.
Art as memory.
Art as survival.

Jaqulin Joseph is a New Orleans–area visual artist, photographer, sculptor, author and educator whose practice moves across painting, mixed media, assemblage, bronze sculpture, photography and writing.

Her work is rooted in emotion, memory, observation, identity and transformation. She is especially interested in womanhood, ancestry, resilience, belonging, grief, history and the ways personal experience becomes physical material.

Joseph holds a Bachelor of Arts in Fine Arts from the University of New Orleans. Her professional life has included work as an art instructor, photographer, gallery director and owner, educator and community-engagement professional.

She founded and operated Abstrax Arts Gallery and Photography Studio in San Diego, developing exhibitions, connecting artists with buyers, teaching art and creating visual communications. Whether making an image, building a sculpture, writing a book or teaching, she approaches creativity as an act of truth-telling, discovery and connection.
