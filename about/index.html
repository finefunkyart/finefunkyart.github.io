About the artist

Jaqulin Joseph

Artist, photographer, sculptor, author, and educator.

Jaqulin Joseph is a contemporary visual artist and the creative force behind Fine Funky Art. Working across abstraction, mixed media, painting, photography, and experimental processes, she creates art that is bold, layered, expressive, and unmistakably alive.

With a background in fine arts and decades of creative practice, Jaqulin approaches the canvas with curiosity rather than rigid rules. Color, texture, line, memory, movement, and emotion often collide in her work. Some pieces are loud and unapologetic; others reveal themselves quietly through subtle surfaces and unexpected details.

Her work ranges from vibrant abstract compositions to pieces influenced by nature, personal history, culture, place, and the ordinary objects and experiences that surround us. Rather than creating simply to match a room, Jaqulin believes art should give a room something to say.

She is also an art educator and the founder of Fine Funky Art, a creative brand built around the idea that meaningful art does not have to behave itself.

Jaqulin Joseph lives and creates in the New Orleans area.
