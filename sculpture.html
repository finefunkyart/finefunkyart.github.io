Bronze sculpture

Form, weight, presence.

Jaqulin Joseph's bronze works explore independence, intimacy, tension, and the human figure through compact, enduring forms. Each sculpture is presented as its own body of work, separate from the painting and mixed-media collection.

Additional dimensions, edition information, availability, and exhibition details are supplied with serious inquiries.
