Private collection · photographic history

Vintage Cameras

A small museum of machines that once held light. These cameras trace more than a century of changing materials, mechanics, portability, and the desire to preserve a moment.

Collection catalog

Objects & histories

Each camera is presented as an object of photographic history. Additional photographs, condition details, and availability are supplied on request.

Collection 01

Rochester Premo B

Large-format folding camera · c. 1890s–early 1900s

Built by the Rochester Optical Company in Rochester, New York, the Premo family belongs to the early age of portable large-format photography. Its wooden body, bellows, and metal fittings reflect a period when the camera was both precision instrument and finely made object.
