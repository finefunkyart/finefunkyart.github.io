Rochester Premo B

4 × 5 folding camera

Maker
Rochester Optical Company · Rochester, New York
Model
Premo B
Era
c. 1894–1902
Format
4 × 5 in
Shutter
Victor shutter
Material
Wood, leather bellows, brass hardware
Condition
Vintage condition with visible age, wear, and original character

An object from the first age of portable photography. The Premo line was introduced by the Rochester Optical Company in the 1890s and became one of its best-known camera families. The Premo B was offered as a well-made hand and tripod camera for serious amateurs, combining a wooden body, folding construction, rising and falling front, and plate or film capability.
