Collection 01

POCO Folding Camera

Wooden folding camera · exact model, format, and date under research

The surviving camera shown here is identified by the POCO nameplate on the front standard. Visible features include a polished wooden body, deep burgundy bellows, a folding front bed with focusing track, metal lens-and-shutter hardware, side support struts, a leather-covered outer case, and a top carrying handle. The wear, scuffs, exposed edges, aged bellows, and hardware are intentionally shown as part of the camera’s present condition. The “Oliva Serie V Melanio” lettering visible beneath the camera belongs to the display box and is not camera branding. No exact model, film/plate format, or production year is being claimed until further markings or measurements confirm them.
